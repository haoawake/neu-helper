# v3.2.1 —— 海边是装着海浪的玻璃球，像素是一个草方块

3.2.0 那两档只换了球的**配色**。这一版换的是**画的东西**。

## 海边:一颗装着水的玻璃球

球里装到六成满，水面是两条正弦叠出来的浪 —— 频率 3.1 和 6.7。只用一条的话
晃起来像钟摆，叠一条快的才有"水在荡"的乱劲。浪尖一道白沫，上沿比下沿薄，
看着才像光打在浪尖上。

水这一层画在**玻璃里、镜面高光下面**。顺序错了就成了"水面浮着一块白斑"，
而不是"隔着玻璃看见里面的水"。

球里原本那两团流转的光留着，它们在水面**之上** —— 转到某个相位，暖色那团升到
水面上方，像海上的日头。

## 像素:我的世界的草方块

这一档不画球了，画一个等距立方体：顶面草绿、两个侧面泥土（左暗右亮，立体感全靠
这个，不是靠描边）、交界处一圈参差的草沿。

- 全部按 **16 格**的网格取色（草方块贴图的原始分辨率），一个渐变都没有
- 杂色用**坐标哈希**而不是 `random` —— 每帧重摇的话那不是泥土纹理，是雪花屏
- 只有外轮廓做抗锯齿。内部要是也抹平了，那就不叫像素风叫没画完
- 投影也换成方的：像素游戏里的影子就是底下垫一块深色，不是柔光

## In English

3.2.0 only recolored the orb for these two themes. This one changes **what is
drawn**.

**Beach** is a glass ball with water in it, filled about 60%. The surface is two
sine waves (frequencies 3.1 and 6.7) — one alone swings like a pendulum; the
faster one on top is what makes it slosh. There is foam along the crest, thinner
above than below, the way light catches a wave. The water sits *inside* the glass
and *under* the specular highlight; the other order would look like a white blob
floating on the surface. The two drifting lights stay, above the waterline — at
some phases the warm one rises like a sun over the sea.

**Pixel** does not draw a ball at all. It draws an isometric Minecraft grass
block: green top, dirt sides (left darker than right — that is the entire source
of depth, no outlines), and a ragged grass lip where they meet. Everything is
quantised to a 16-cell grid with no gradients anywhere, and the speckle comes from
a coordinate hash rather than `random`, because re-rolling it every frame would be
static, not soil. Only the outer silhouette is anti-aliased; smoothing the inside
would defeat the point.
